Where is the load cell

Jog the swab tip over the middle of the cell and capture. "Go to" moves X and Y only, at whatever Z you are at. The start Z only needs to be a few mm above the cell: the probe finds the real height itself by stepping down until the force reads the target.

Probe test

Order: rise to the start Z if below it, move over the cell at that height, settle onto the start Z, tare, then step down at the descent speed. Z never moves at travel speed. Each step waits for the move to finish and a force reading, so the average speed is a little below the setting, most visibly with small steps.

Load cell to glass offset

The load cell sits above the glass. A job finds the swab's contact height on the cell, then adds this offset to get the scribe height on the slide. Use the same swab for both touches.

  1. Contact on the load cell
    Cell contact Znot set

    Probe on the Load cell tab, or jog the swab down onto the cell by hand until it just reads force.

  2. Contact on the glass
    Glass contact Znot set

    Jog over the slide, lower in 0.05 to 0.1 mm steps until the swab just touches the glass, then capture. Stay above the Z floor.

  3. Offset
    Glass − cell–Locked value–

    Scribe Z = cell contact Z + offset. The offset is normally negative because the glass is lower than the cell.

Gripper servo

First run: no swab in the jaws, jaws clear of everything. The very first command after power-up jumps (the horn's resting spot is unknown), so start it near the middle of the window. Every move after that ramps at the chosen speed and cannot leave the window. Widen the window only in small steps.

Live footage

The stream runs only while this tab is open. The stamp burned into the bottom-left says REAL, MOCK or REMOTE, the time, and the size of the source frame, so a frozen picture or a mocked camera is obvious. Focus is the manual ring on the lens. Lower the width or frame rate if the picture lags over Wi-Fi.

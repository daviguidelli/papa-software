Where is the load cell

Jog the swab tip over the middle of the cell and capture. "Go to" moves X and Y only, at whatever Z you are at. The start Z only needs to be a few mm above the cell: the probe finds the real height itself by stepping down until the force reads the target.

Probe test

Load cell to glass offset

The load cell sits above the glass. A job finds the swab's contact height on the cell, then adds this offset to get the scribe height on the slide. Use the same swab for both touches.

  1. Contact on the load cell
    Cell contact Znot set

    Probe on the Load cell tab, or jog the swab down onto the cell by hand until it just reads force.

  2. Contact on the glass
    Glass contact Znot set

    Jog over the slide, lower in 0.05 to 0.1 mm steps until the swab just touches the glass, then capture. Stay above the Z floor.

  3. Offset
    Glass − cell–Locked value–

    Scribe Z = cell contact Z + offset. The offset is normally negative because the glass is lower than the cell.

Z reference · no homing needed

More info

Klipper will not move Z until it thinks Z is homed. This tells it "Z is the number above, right here", with no motion. The number is a label you choose, not a measured height. It clears the floor (the old one belonged to the old label) and sets a ceiling above it. Home X and Y first. Do this again after any firmware restart.

More info

Where is the load cell
More info

Jog the swab tip over the middle of the cell and capture. "Go to" moves X and Y only, at whatever Z you are at. The start Z only needs to be a few mm above the cell: the probe finds the real height itself by stepping down until the force reads the target.

Probe test

More info

Order: rise to the start Z if below it, move over the cell at that height, settle onto the start Z, tare, then step down at the descent speed. Z never moves at travel speed. Each step waits for the move to finish and a force reading, so the average speed is a little below the setting, most visibly with small steps.

Load cell to glass offset

More info

The load cell sits above the glass. A job finds the swab's contact height on the cell, then adds this offset to get the scribe height on the slide. Use the same swab for both touches.

  1. Contact on the load cell
    Cell contact Znot set

    Probe on the Load cell tab, or jog the swab down onto the cell by hand until it just reads force.

  2. Contact on the glass
    Glass contact Znot set
    More info

    Jog over the slide, lower in 0.05 to 0.1 mm steps until the swab just touches the glass, then capture. Stay above the Z floor.

  3. Offset
    Glass − cell–Locked value–

    Scribe Z = cell contact Z + offset. The offset is normally negative because the glass is lower than the cell.

Gripper servo

First run: no swab in the jaws, jaws clear of everything. The very first command after power-up jumps (the horn's resting spot is unknown), so start it near the middle of the window. Every move after that ramps at the chosen speed and cannot leave the window. Widen the window only in small steps.

Live footage

More info

The stream runs only while this tab is open. The stamp burned into the bottom-left says REAL, MOCK or REMOTE, the time, and the size of the source frame, so a frozen picture or a mocked camera is obvious. Focus is the manual ring on the lens. One-button actions

Grip and dispose

Each button works from wherever the gantry is. Z rises to the safe height, then XY travels, then Z lowers onto the target.

E-STOP in the header halts a move already under way.

    Full cycle

    Routine 1

    Grips a swab, scrubs the sketch from the Scrub tab, then disposes of the swab. Needs the taught positions below and a Scrub start, sketch and floor. Contact Z for the scrub start should be set with a swab in the jaws.

    A stop or fault during the scrub lifts the swab clear and keeps it in the jaws. It does not go to the bin.

      Taught positions

      Jog there with the controls on the left, then press Save here. Home X and Y first.

      More info

      Pickup is where the open jaws close around a swab. Bin is the centre of the bin, where the jaws open. Safe Z is the height XY moves at: it must be at or above both of those Z values and clear of everything on the bed. After a grip the swab rises by the lift below, then moves to the centre of the gantry.

      Motion settings

      While the jaws close, the gantry moves this far in +X so the swab meets the jaw at the centre. 0 turns it off. Negative moves −X.

      Checks

      A substrate whose measured array differs from its pattern by more than the tolerance, or is turned more than the largest turn, is flagged and will not run unless you confirm it. Arrays must sit within 45° of the nominal orientation for the top-left corner to be found.
